About SwarmEnergy

SwarmEnergy combines AI compute with energy sovereignty. Our air-gapped infrastructure provides ultimate security and privacy, with no vendor lock-in and no middleman. We offer decentralized compute, Proof of Compute (PoC) validation, and cutting-edge AI-powered workstations.

All our compute resources are locally owned and operated, managed within our controlled datacenter environments. This means no middleman, no outsourcing – we own the compute, and we manage the rails, ensuring complete sovereign trust and control over your data and operations.

SwarmCode.eth

SwarmCode.eth is the central hub of your decentralized AI and compute ecosystem. It serves as a code repository, deployment system, and smart contract framework designed for building, managing, and executing decentralized compute workloads across your network of independent nodes (e.g., BeeBoxes, Zima-2 devices, GPU rigs, etc.).

SwarmCode.eth provides tools, libraries, and a set of protocols that allow clients to deploy custom AI models, manage compute resources, and interact with the SwarmEnergy infrastructure. It's the core infrastructure that connects the various agents, devices, and services across the SwarmOS ecosystem, making it easy for clients to tap into sovereign compute in a secure, efficient, and trustless way.

Key Features of SwarmCode.eth:

Decentralized Compute Deployment

  • Clients can deploy their own AI models, scripts, or workloads onto your network of edge devices (like Zima-2 or GPU-powered machines) without relying on centralized cloud providers.

Resource Management

  • SwarmCode.eth provides detailed analytics and resource allocation tools, allowing clients to efficiently manage their compute power, GPU resources, and energy consumption.

SwarmOS Ecosystem Snapshot

1. SwarmOS - The Proof of Compute (PoC) Layer

SwarmOS is the core operating system and ecosystem that powers the entire Swarm. It serves as the foundational platform that enables decentralized, energy-efficient compute services across a network of independent agents. SwarmOS is responsible for orchestrating the compute tasks, managing decentralized resources, and ensuring data sovereignty, security, and privacy.

Key Features:

  • Proof of Compute (PoC): Ensures every compute task is verifiable and auditable via blockchain-based proofs.
  • Distributed Compute Management: Manages compute nodes, GPUs, and resources across your ecosystem.
  • Sovereign Identity: Each agent, client, and device is linked to an ENS identity, ensuring complete control and trustlessness.
  • Integration with Decentralized Energy: Tightly integrated with SwarmEnergy, leveraging energy-efficient compute to power tasks.
  • API and SDKs: Provides tools for developers to build and deploy decentralized applications.

2. SwarmCompute - On-Demand Decentralized Compute

SwarmCompute is a decentralized compute marketplace where users can access on-demand computing resources. Powered by SwarmOS, it allows clients to rent GPU power, storage, and AI workstations from independent operators, eliminating the need for centralized cloud providers. SwarmCompute offers flexible billing based on energy usage and compute time.

Compute Offerings:

  • High-Performance GPUs: 48x 5090s (32GB), 48x RTX 6000 (96GB), 128x 3090Ti/3090
  • SwarmBox Minis: Custom AI agent boxes with 4GB GPU, N150 CPU, and 64GB RAM.

Transparent Billing:

  • Pricing is based on kWh usage, with $0.0825 per kWh and a $0.02 network fee for each transaction.
  • Example: A 100 kWh medical imaging task would cost approximately $8.27.

Key Features:

  • On-Demand Compute Resources: Clients can rent GPUs, storage, and processing power, paying only for what they use.
  • Decentralized Resource Sharing: Computes power is provided by a network of independent devices, including SwarmBox Pro Mini devices, high-performance GPUs, and edge nodes.
  • Task Automation: Clients can schedule and automate tasks such as AI model training, image processing, or any compute-intensive workload.
  • Security and Privacy: All tasks are run on air-gapped infrastructure for added security and privacy.

3. SwarmEnergy - Decentralized, Energy-Efficient Compute Power

SwarmEnergy is the backbone of your decentralized ecosystem, providing energy-efficient compute power for Swarm nodes and agents. It uses local energy sources, such as solar or other green energy, and ensures that compute power is used in a sustainable, efficient manner. It works in synergy with SwarmOS to manage and distribute power across the network.

Key Features:

  • Energy-Efficient Compute: Energy-efficient algorithms and local, renewable energy sources to run compute workloads.
  • Decentralized Energy Distribution: Allows clients to harness decentralized compute power, minimizing reliance on large, centralized data centers.
  • Billing Based on Energy Consumption: Clients are billed based on kWh usage, promoting transparency and environmental sustainability.
  • Integration with AI Workstations: Provides energy to AI workstations and edge devices like SwarmBox Pro Mini.

4. SwarmBox Pro Mini - Decentralized Edge Device for Sovereign Compute

SwarmBox Pro Mini is a small, powerful edge device designed for decentralized compute. It acts as a mini agent within the Swarm ecosystem, providing compute, storage, and AI inference capabilities. SwarmBox Pro Mini devices are used to run AI models, process data, and contribute to the decentralized compute power of SwarmCompute.

Key Features:

  • Compact Edge Device: Small form factor, ideal for home, office, or enterprise-level compute tasks.
  • High Performance: Equipped with GPUs, these devices deliver powerful compute for AI workloads, including medical imaging, data analysis, and AI model inference.
  • Air-Gapped Security: Ensures complete data privacy by running tasks in isolated, secure environments.
  • Energy-Efficient: Designed to work in tandem with SwarmEnergy, using low-power and renewable energy sources.
  • Swarm Integration: Each SwarmBox Pro Mini is an active participant in the Swarm ecosystem, providing compute power and consuming decentralized energy.

How Clients Use the Ecosystem:

  • Deploying AI Workloads with SwarmCompute: Clients can use SwarmCompute to rent GPU compute power on-demand to run AI models, process data, and train algorithms. They can schedule compute tasks, scale resources as needed, and pay based on actual energy consumption and compute time.
  • Running Decentralized Edge Compute with SwarmBox Pro Mini: Clients can set up SwarmBox Pro Mini devices at their locations to run edge AI workloads and contribute to the Swarm by offering their idle compute resources to others in the network. These mini agents run tasks in air-gapped environments, ensuring high privacy and security for sensitive data.
  • Energy-Efficient, Sovereign Computing: Clients can take advantage of SwarmEnergy's decentralized energy model, which allows them to run their compute tasks using local renewable energy, reducing costs and increasing sustainability. They can connect their SwarmBox Pro Mini to the network and start earning rewards by providing compute resources while consuming energy efficiently.
  • Optimizing with SwarmOS: Clients access SwarmOS to manage their decentralized AI workflows and compute tasks. They can fine-tune AI models, monitor performance, and interact with other Swarm services, all while maintaining full control over their data and resources.

Visualizing the Ecosystem:

  • SwarmOS acts as the central orchestrator, connecting all devices and services in the ecosystem.
  • SwarmCompute allows clients to access on-demand compute power, scaling their needs in real-time.
  • SwarmEnergy powers the system by providing energy-efficient, decentralized compute resources.
  • SwarmBox Pro Mini acts as the edge device, contributing compute power and processing workloads locally, with security and privacy as top priorities.

In Summary:

The SwarmOS ecosystem offers a complete, decentralized solution for AI workloads and compute, powered by SwarmEnergy. With SwarmCompute, clients can access on-demand compute resources, while SwarmBox Pro Mini devices allow individuals and organizations to contribute compute power. The entire system runs on air-gapped infrastructure, ensuring data sovereignty and security. Clients can scale their operations easily, benefit from energy-efficient computing, and maintain full control over their data.
