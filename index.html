About SwarmEnergy

SwarmEnergy combines AI compute with energy sovereignty. Our air-gapped infrastructure provides ultimate security and privacy, with no vendor lock-in and no middleman. We offer decentralized compute, Proof of Compute (PoC) validation, and cutting-edge AI-powered workstations.

Our Pricing

We believe in transparent, fair pricing, charged in USDC via swarmusdc.eth. You only pay for the energy you consume.

  • Energy Usage: $0.0825 per kWh
  • Network Fee: $0.02 per hour

Example Pricing: Medical Image Processing

For a task requiring 100 kWh of energy:

  • Energy Cost: 100 kWh * $0.0825/kWh = $8.25
  • Network Fee (e.g., for 1 hour): 1 hour * $0.02/hour = $0.02
  • Total Estimated Cost: $8.27
